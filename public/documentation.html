Brand Overview

Earthy Concepts is a modern Ayurvedic wellness brand born in Dubai, UAE — where the ancient meets the avant-garde. The brand blends indigenous knowledge systems with modern wellness practices.

Brand Philosophy

  • Sells wellness concepts practiced through simple daily routines
  • Products are the tools that enable these concepts
  • Does NOT make medical claims or sell quick fixes

Key Problem Areas Addressed

  1. Stress & poor sleep
  2. Hormonal balance
  3. Immunity
  4. Blood sugar & metabolism
  5. Skin & glow
  6. Long-term heart wellness

Brand Tone

Calm Premium Trust-building Educational Modern

Website Structure

Pages

  1. Home - Conversion-focused landing page with hero, bestsellers, concept tiles, bundles, testimonials, and trust badges
  2. Shop - All products with category/concept filters and sorting options
  3. Product Detail - Full product information with ingredients, usage instructions, and reviews
  4. Bundles - Concept packs with savings displayed
  5. Concepts - Shop by wellness concern with concept tiles
  6. Concept Detail - Individual concept pages with problem/solution and recommended products
  7. About - Brand story, mission, values, and quality commitments

Compliance & Language Guidelines

✓ Approved Language

  • "Supports..."
  • "Helps maintain..."
  • "Designed for daily use..."
  • "Traditional herbs used for..."
  • "As part of a balanced lifestyle..."

✗ Prohibited Language

  • "Cures..."
  • "Treats..."
  • "Diagnoses..."
  • "Guaranteed results..."
  • "Medical-grade..."
