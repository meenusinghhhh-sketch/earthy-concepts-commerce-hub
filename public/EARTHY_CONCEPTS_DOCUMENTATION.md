# EARTHY CONCEPTS - E-Commerce Website Documentation

## Brand Overview

**Earthy Concepts** is a modern Ayurvedic wellness brand born in Dubai, UAE — where the ancient meets the avant-garde. The brand blends indigenous knowledge systems with modern wellness practices.

### Brand Philosophy
- Sells **wellness concepts** practiced through simple daily routines
- Products are the **tools** that enable these concepts
- Does NOT make medical claims or sell quick fixes

### Key Problem Areas Addressed
1. Stress & poor sleep
2. Hormonal balance
3. Immunity
4. Blood sugar & metabolism
5. Skin & glow
6. Long-term heart wellness

### Brand Tone
- Calm
- Premium
- Trust-building
- Educational (not medical)
- Modern, not spiritual
